Honors Engineering Capstone

A year-long original engineering project — researched, prototyped, and defended before a panel of real engineers.

Grades 11–12
Credits 5
Length Full Year
Prerequisite POE or CEA
Pathway PLTW Engineering

About the Course

Honors Engineering Capstone (PLTW-Capstone) is the culminating course in the Project Lead The Way high school Engineering sequence, offering students a premier, immersive experience in applied engineering. In this honors-level capstone, students work in collaborative teams to identify, research, and solve a complex, open-ended technical problem, applying the full engineering design process from concept to prototype.

Students conduct rigorous research to validate and justify their chosen problem, then design, build, and test innovative solutions. Throughout the process, they receive mentorship from industry professionals and higher education partners, providing invaluable real-world guidance and insight into advanced engineering practices. The course emphasizes professional skills including team collaboration, technical communication, project management, and critical analysis.

Student teams culminate their work by presenting and defending their original solutions to an external panel of experts, mirroring professional engineering review and evaluation. Honors Capstone prepares students for postsecondary engineering study and careers, offering unparalleled opportunities to connect academic learning with industry and higher education.

Why Take Capstone

  • Define and solve an original engineering problem of your own choosing — concept to working prototype.
  • Get mentored by industry professionals and university partners throughout the year.
  • Defend your finished solution before an external panel of practicing engineers.
  • Graduate with a portfolio project that genuinely stands out on college applications.
  • PLTW coursework can earn industry-recognized credentials and college opportunities through PLTW ACE partner schools.
  • Build project-management, technical-writing, and team-leadership skills you'll use in any career.
